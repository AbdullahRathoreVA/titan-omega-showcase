## FAQ
**Is this live? Can I try it?** Yes — there’s a live demo you can fly through right now (link above).
**Does it really run autonomously?** It researches, decides with confidence scores, and drafts real work continuously. Anything sensitive (like code changes) is review-gated — a human approves before it ships. Autonomy with a seatbelt.
**Why does the revenue say $0?** Because it’s real. TITAN never fakes a number — the counter only moves when real money moves. That honesty is the point.
**Is my data safe?** Runs on your own deploy. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you connect an AI key; then only your prompts go to that provider.
**Do I need to be technical?** To use a deployed instance, no. To self-host, you’ll follow a documented setup — or I’ll deploy and brand it for you.
**Is it multi-tenant SaaS?** Not today — it’s single-tenant (one business per deploy), by design. If you want it deployed for your business, that’s exactly the service I offer.
**What does it cost?** See options below — a deploy-for-you service, a founder license, or an agency (white-label) license.
**Who built it?** One person — Muhammad Abdullah Rathore — solo, on a $0 budget, with AI pair-programming.
