# 02 — Short-Form Video Library (24 concepts)

Reels / YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Facebook Reels. Every concept is buildable from your existing showcase footage using the clip labels defined in `00_RESEARCH_POSITIONING.md` (`BOOT`, `UNIVERSE`, `WARROOM`, `FACTORY`, `FINANCE`, etc.). Confirm your real timestamps once and every script here becomes shoot-ready.

**How to use:** Pick 3–5 per week, matching the style to the platform (see `09_CALENDAR_AND_QC.md`). Keep each 20–45s. Burn in captions. Always end on a destination.

**Legend per card:** Style · Audience · Goal · Emotion · Hook · Script · Clips · On-screen · Music/SFX · CTA · Hashtags · Why it works · A/B.

## 🎬 Cinematic Trailer

### 1. "Not a dashboard. A universe."
- **Audience:** AI builders, design-driven founders · **Goal:** Awe + demo clicks · **Emotion:** Awe.
- **Hook (0–2s):** “Every other AI tool is a spreadsheet. This is a galaxy.”
- **Script:** “This is TITAN Ω. Twelve divisions. A hundred and two agents. Every glowing thing you see is *real* work happening right now. Not a mock-up. A living command center — built by one person.”
- **Clips:** `BOOT` → `FLYIN` → `UNIVERSE` orbit → `GALAXY` click → hold on hologram.
- **On-screen:** Minimal. Only the closing line “TITAN Ω — the AI that runs a business.”
- **Music/SFX:** Full trailer track, one big drop on `FLYIN`. · **CTA:** “Fly through it → link in bio.”
- **Hashtags:** #AI #buildinpublic #threejs #startup #futuretech
- **Why it works:** Pure spectacle + “it’s all real” subverts demo skepticism. · **A/B:** Silent+music vs. VO.

### 2. "System online."
- **Audience:** General tech · **Goal:** Retention/rewatch · **Emotion:** Anticipation.
- **Hook:** The `BOOT` particles assembling + the AI voice “systems online” — no added words for 3s.
- **Script:** *(let the boot play)* … then: “When I open my business every morning, this is what boots up.”
- **Clips:** Full `BOOT` → `FLYIN`. · **On-screen:** “POV: you run a company of 102 AI agents.”

- **Why it works:** POV framing + a genuinely cinematic cold open = high 3s retention. · **A/B:** “POV” vs. “This boots up every morning.”

## Production notes for the whole library
- **Reusable intro (2s):** the `BOOT` particles are your brand ident — open ~1 in 3 videos with it for recognition.
- **Reusable outro (2s):** `UNIVERSE` hero + `TITAN Ω` + demo URL card.
- **Caption discipline:** every video burns in captions; first line = the hook verbatim.
- **One destination per video:** rotate demo link / GitHub star / DM keyword so you’re not always asking for the same action.
- **Repurpose ratio:** each concept → 1 Reel + 1 Short + 1 TikTok + 1 FB Reel = 4 posts from one edit.
