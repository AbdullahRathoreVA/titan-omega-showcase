# 01 — The Flagship Short  ⭐ (produce this first)

This is your "I need the reel no matter what" asset: one 35-second vertical video engineered to stop the scroll, show the wow, land the honesty hook, and send people to the demo. Everything is specced so you (or an editor) can cut it in an afternoon from your existing showcase footage.

> Format: 9:16 vertical · 30–38 seconds · works as YouTube Short, Instagram Reel, TikTok, and Facebook Reel with no changes.

---

## The concept: "The AI dashboard that refuses to lie"

**Target audience:** Indie founders, AI builders, agency owners (and the huge "how did one person build this" crowd).
**Goal:** Demo visits + GitHub stars + "who built this?" comments.
**Emotional triggers:** Awe (the visuals) → curiosity (agents thinking) → surprise + trust (the $0 honesty twist) → admiration (solo, $0).
**Why it works:** It front-loads the most cinematic 2 seconds you own, then subverts the "AI dashboard" cliché with a genuinely counter-intuitive flex — *it shows $0 on purpose.* Pattern interrupt + real substance + a rootable founder = high completion and high comment rate.

## The 1–3 second hook — 5 A/B variants (test these)

Swap only shot 1's text + VO; keep the rest identical. Post one every few days and let retention pick the winner.

- **A (Confession):** "I gave an AI control of my business."
- **B (Contrarian flex):** "This AI dashboard shows $0 revenue — on purpose."
- **C (Underdog):** "Solo. $0 budget. 102 AI agents. Pakistan."

> Data rule: judge on **3-second retention** and **average watch %**, not likes. Whichever hook keeps >60% at 3s wins the next 5 posts.

## Editing instructions

- **Aspect / safe zones:** 1080×1920. Keep text between 12% and 82% height (clear of the caption bar and the top UI).
- **Cut pace:** Fast but legible — 3–6s per shot, hard cuts on the beat. No slow dissolves except the `FLYIN` whoosh (shot 2).
- **The money beat:** On shot 6, punch in ~10% on the `$0` figure and **duck the music to near-silence for ~0.6s** so "$0" lands in a pocket. This silence is the most important edit in the video.
- **Text style:** Bold condensed sans (e.g. a Montserrat/Anton-style face), white with a subtle glow to match the neon UI. One line at a time. Animate in with a fast 6–8 frame rise + fade.
- **Color:** Lean into the existing purple→blue bloom. Add a faint vignette so text pops.
- **Captions:** Burn in auto-captions (85% of feed views are muted) — but the VO track should still carry it for sound-on viewers.

## Camera-movement suggestions (from source footage)
- Prefer the **orbiting/breathing** camera moves already in `UNIVERSE` for shots 2–3 — they read as premium.
- For the `FINANCE` beat, a slow **push-in** on the $0 figure adds gravity. If your footage is static there, add a subtle 105%→110% digital push in the edit.

## Music & SFX
- **Music style:** Cinematic tech / "trailer minimal" — a track with a clear drop around 0:07 and a dip you can exploit at 0:22. Think dark-synth + pulse, not lo-fi.
- **SFX kit:** UI chime (boot), deep whoosh (fly-in), six rapid clicks (Content Factory), one low sub-hit on "$0," a final resolve chime on the CTA.

## Why this concept should engage (the mechanics)
- **Hook = pattern interrupt.** "I gave an AI control of my business" is a stakes statement; the visual immediately over-delivers.
- **The $0 twist is a genuine 'wait, what?' beat** — surprise is the most reliable driver of comments and shares, and here it's *true*, so it survives scrutiny.
- **Rewatch value:** the universe visuals reward a second look, lifting average view duration.
- **Comment engine:** the "would you trust an AI?" question is polarizing but safe — it reliably produces takes.

## Variations for A/B testing (beyond the hook)
1. **VO vs. text-only + music** (some audiences convert better silent).
2. **Ending on `FINANCE` $0** vs. ending on `VOICE` 12-languages — test which CTA card gets more clicks.
3. **35s vs. 22s hyper-cut** — the 22s version drops shots 5 and 7.
4. **Founder-on-camera cold open** (2s of you talking to camera) spliced before shot 1 — face-first openers often lift trust and retention.
