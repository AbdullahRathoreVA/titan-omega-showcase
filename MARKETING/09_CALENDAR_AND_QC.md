# 09 — 30-Day Calendar & Quality Control

---

## How the funnel actually works (set expectations honestly)

Attention (short-form) → Credibility (LinkedIn + long-form) → Consideration (demo visit, GitHub star) → Conversation (DM/email) → Deal (deploy service / license).

No single post “goes viral and makes you rich.” The system compounds: consistent honest proof-of-work builds an audience and a reputation, and *conversations* — not view counts — turn into the $300–$1,500 deploys and $99–$799 licenses in your playbook. Your playbook’s own honest target: **$500–$2k/mo within 3–6 months if distribution is worked daily.** This calendar is that daily work.

## 30-day launch calendar (mirrors your playbook’s 4-week plan)

**Daily baseline (every single day):** 1 short-form video (from `02`), 5 meaningful comments in your niche, 10 warm DMs/outreach with value (not a pitch).

### Week 1 — Go live & establish the story
- **Mon:** Flagship short (`01`) on all platforms. LinkedIn Post 1 (launch). X anchor thread (`05`).
- **Tue:** Short #2 (Cinematic “System online”). LinkedIn comment sprint. IG Story “morning boot.”
- **Wed:** Short #3 (Founder “$0 and a laptop”). LinkedIn Post 2 (lesson). X short posts x2.
- **Thu:** Short #5 (War Room). IG Carousel 1. Reply to 10 “AI agent” threads on X.
- **Fri:** Short #6 (Content Factory). LinkedIn Post 3 (engineering). Publish YouTube flagship tour (`03`).
- **Sat:** Short #15 (Myth vs reality). X build-log update. IG “Ask the agents” Story.
- **Sun:** Short #19 (Luxury/aesthetic). Plan next week. Light engagement only.

### Week 2 — Depth & first conversations
- Ship Shorts #4, #7, #8, #10, #11, #17, #21 across the week.
- LinkedIn: Post 4 (leadership), Post 5 (AI trend), + Carousel.
- X: failover thread repurpose, 2 polls.
- **Publish YouTube video 2 (three.js build).**
- Start Email sequence for anyone who DM’d. First soft offer (LinkedIn Post 6) at week’s end.

### Week 3 — Proof & authority
- Shorts #12, #13, #16, #18, #20, #24.
- LinkedIn: a case-study-style post (“what happened when I dispatched a real problem to a division”). Raise any freelance rate.
- **Publish YouTube video 3 (agentic AI / real PRs).**
- Post the mini-doc (#23) as a Reel + full cut on YouTube.
- Capture first testimonial (offer 1–2 free/discounted deploys in exchange for a review).

### Week 4 — Convert & compound
- Shorts #9, #14, #22 + re-cut the best performer from weeks 1–3 with a new hook.
- LinkedIn: results/build-in-public recap post + a clear offer post.
- X: “month of building in public — honest numbers” thread (real stats only).
- Line up a Product Hunt launch *only if* a checkout + a testimonial exist (per playbook).
- Review analytics; double down on the 2–3 formats that actually drove demo visits/DMs.

**Every week, always:** ship one visible product improvement and post the clip of it. That single habit is the engine.

## Repurposing map (1 idea → many assets)
One feature (e.g. War Room) → 1 Reel/Short/TikTok/FB Reel (same edit) + 1 LinkedIn post + 1 X short + 1 IG carousel slide + 1 chapter in the YouTube tour. **Five source ideas can fill two weeks.**

## Quality-control scorecard (every asset in this system was checked against it)

Score each asset 1–5 on:
1. **Clarity** — is the one idea obvious in 3 seconds?
2. **Originality** — does it avoid generic AI-hype phrasing?
3. **Credibility** — is every claim true and traceable to the repos?
4. **Emotional impact** — awe, curiosity, surprise, or inspiration present?
5. **Shareability** — would someone send this to a friend or save it?
6. **Conversion** — is there exactly one clear next step?

**Rule:** anything under 4 on Credibility gets cut, not softened. Anything under 3 on Clarity or Conversion gets rewritten.

### Self-review notes applied to this system
- **Strengthened:** every “wow” claim was traced back to a verified feature (102 agents, 12 languages, triple-provider failover, 32 tests). Nothing aspirational is stated as present-tense fact.
- **The honesty angle** was promoted from a footnote to the lead hook — it’s the single most differentiated, credible, and share-worthy thing you own.
- **Every asset has one destination** (demo / GitHub / DM) so attention converts.
- **Known weaknesses are stated, not hidden** (single-tenant, $0 revenue, no SaaS billing) — which paradoxically raises trust and pre-qualifies buyers.
- **Removed** any implied guarantee of views or revenue; replaced with the playbook’s honest, believable trajectory.

## The three metrics that actually matter (ignore vanity)
1. **Demo visits** (are people flying through it?).
2. **Qualified conversations** (DMs/emails from real potential buyers).
3. **GitHub stars + follower growth on your primary channel (LinkedIn).**
Track these weekly. Views are a leading indicator; these are the ones that pay.
