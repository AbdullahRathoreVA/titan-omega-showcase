## Post 2 — Startup lesson
**Headline:** The most valuable thing I built wasn’t a feature. It was a rule.

**Body:**
> “Never fake a number.”
>
> Every AI demo I saw hard-coded beautiful metrics. It would’ve been easy to do the same — investors and clients love a dashboard full of green.
>
> I did the opposite, and it was harder: real ledgers, run-rate labels, honest empty states.
>
> Here’s what that constraint did — it made every feature better. If you can’t fake the number, you have to actually make the thing work.
>
> Honesty isn’t just ethics. It’s a design forcing-function.

**CTA:** “What’s a constraint that quietly made your product better?”
**Hashtags:** #productdevelopment #startups #buildinpublic #AI

## Post 3 — Engineering insight
**Headline:** At 2am, an AI provider retired a model. My app didn’t even blink.

**Body:**
> Most AI apps hard-wire one provider. The day that provider changes a model name, they break — silently.
>
> TITAN chains three: Groq → Gemini → OpenRouter, and discovers each one’s live model catalog at runtime. If one fails or retires a model, it fails over automatically.
>
> I also added one boring endpoint I now can’t live without: `/api/doctor` — it shows exactly what the running container sees (which keys resolved, which providers are live). When something breaks, I don’t guess. I open one URL.
>
> Reliability isn’t a feature you add later. It’s a posture.

**CTA:** “What’s your ‘/doctor’ — the unglamorous tool that saves you every week?”
**Hashtags:** #softwareengineering #AI #devops #buildinpublic
