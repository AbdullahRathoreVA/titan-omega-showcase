# 03 — Long-Form YouTube

Long-form is where the *credibility* and *SEO* compound. Shorts win attention; these win trust and search. Ship one every 2–3 weeks; slice each into 4–6 shorts.

## Series ideas (turn the channel into a franchise)
1. **“Building TITAN in Public”** — short weekly devlogs; one shipped improvement + the clip each week (matches the playbook’s “ship one visible improvement, post the clip” cadence).
2. **“Honest AI”** — a POV series calling out fake AI demos and showing the honest alternative; builds a values-based audience.
3. **“One Founder, Zero Dollars”** — how to build serious software solo with AI; each episode = one system (auth, deploy, 3D, agents).
4. **“Ask the Agents”** — viewers comment a business problem; you dispatch it to a division on camera and show what the agents draft.
